This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the Isomantika mobile app (iOS and Android)
and the website isomantika.com, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what rights you have.

We collect as little as we need to run the service. We do not show ads, do not sell your data and do not track you
across other apps or websites.

## Who we are

Isomantika is operated by **Aleksandr Serenko**, a private individual based in Russia ("we", "us"). We are the
controller of your personal data.

Contact for any privacy question or request: **support@isomantika.com**.

## Data we collect

### Your account

You sign in to the app with Google or Apple. From your sign-in provider we receive and store:

- a unique account identifier;
- your email address. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose "Hide My Email", we receive a private relay address
  instead of your real one;
- which provider you used to sign in;
- your name and a link to your profile picture, if Google includes them in the sign-in data. We do not display or
  use them.

We do not receive your Google or Apple password.

### Your readings

When you make a reading, we store: the spread type, your question (if you wrote one, up to 300 characters), the cards
drawn, the interpretation, the app language, and the date and time. We use your readings to show your history and to
count your daily limit.

If you rate an interpretation (👍 or 👎, with optional reasons), we store the rating with the reading, together with
which AI model wrote the interpretation. We use ratings to improve the quality of interpretations.

**Please do not include sensitive information in your questions**, such as health details, information about other
people that they would not want shared, or anything that identifies you. The service does not need it.

### Messages to support

If you write to us from the app, the email includes a technical block that you can see before sending: your account
identifier, the app version, your platform (iOS or Android) and the app language. It helps us find your account and
reproduce problems. We also receive your email address and whatever you write.

### Contact form on the website

If you send a message through the form at isomantika.com/contact, we receive: your name, email address, Telegram username
(optional), the topic, your message and the website language. For spam protection we also process a one-way hash of
your IP address and the time it took to fill in the form. This processing is based on your consent — see the
[Consent to the Processing of Personal Data](/consent/).

### Technical data

Our servers and service providers automatically process technical data needed to deliver the service and keep it
secure: IP address, request time, device and operating system type, and error information. This data is kept in
server logs for a short period.

### App analytics and crash reports

The app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google:

- **Analytics** — which screens you open and what you do in the app: which spreads you choose and complete, errors,
  ratings, which cards and spreads you open in the reference, changes to settings; the app version, device and
  operating system type, language, approximate country, and how you found the app in the store. We do **not** send
  your questions, the interpretations or your email address. Analytics uses an app instance identifier, not your
  account. You can turn analytics off in the app: Settings → About → Share usage statistics.
- **Crash reports** — when the app crashes or runs into an error: details of the error, the app version, device and
  operating system type, and your account identifier, so that we can match a problem you report to support. Crash
  reports are sent even if analytics is turned off, because they are needed to keep the app working.

We do not use the advertising identifier, do not show ads and do not use this data for advertising.

### Website analytics (only with your consent)

If you click "Accept" in the cookie banner on isomantika.com, we use Google Analytics and Yandex Metrica to understand
how the website is used: pages visited, referring site, approximate location (city or country), device and browser
type, and clicks on app store links. If you click "Reject" or do nothing, these services are not loaded at all.
Session recording (Yandex Metrica Webvisor) is turned off.

### Data stored only on your device

Your language, card back and analytics choices are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. The app also stores a
sign-in session token on your device so that you stay signed in.

### What we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, files, payment information or advertising identifier.
The app has no advertising.

## How interpretations are generated

Interpretations are written by an artificial intelligence language model. To create one, we send the model only your
question, the cards drawn, the spread type and the language. **We do not send your name, email address or account
identifier.**

The request goes through OpenRouter, a service that routes it to a model provider (currently the DeepSeek V4.1 Flash model, which OpenRouter may run on servers of different hosting
providers). In our
OpenRouter settings we have excluded providers that use requests to train their models. Providers may keep requests
for a limited time under their own policies to prevent abuse.

## International transfers

We are based in Russia, and our service providers are located in the EU, the USA, China, Russia and other
countries. Your
data may therefore be processed outside your country, including in countries that do not provide the same level of
data protection as yours. For users in the EU, EEA and UK, transfers are necessary to provide the service you asked
for, and where available they rely on safeguards offered by our providers, such as the European Commission's
Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

## How long we keep data

- **Account and readings** — while your account exists.
- **Readings you remove from your history** — deleted from our database within 48 hours.
- **When you delete your account** — your account and all your readings are deleted immediately. If you signed in
  with Apple on an iPhone or iPad, we also revoke the app's access to your Apple ID. If you signed in with Apple on
  Android, you can revoke this access yourself in your Apple ID settings, under Sign in with Apple.
- **Support emails and contact form messages** — one year after our last reply.
- **Server logs** — a short period set by our providers, usually no more than a few weeks.
- **Backups** — deleted data may remain in database backups for a short time until they are overwritten.
- **App analytics** — 14 months.
- **Crash reports** — 90 days.
- **Website analytics** — according to the retention settings of Google Analytics (no more than 14 months) and Yandex
  Metrica.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

- get a copy of your personal data;
- correct inaccurate data;
- delete your data;
- restrict or object to processing;
- receive your data in a portable format;
- withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect processing that happened before);
- lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

**Deleting your account:** in the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. If you cannot access the app, see
[how to delete your account](/delete-account/).

For any other request, write to support@isomantika.com from the email address you used to sign in, or include your
account identifier (you can find it in any email sent to support from the app). We will reply within 30 days.

## Cookies and similar technologies

**Website.** We store your cookie choice and whether you closed the language suggestion in your browser's local
storage — this is needed for the site to work and is not used to track you. Analytics cookies (for example `_ga`,
`_ga_*`, `_ym_*`) are set only after you click "Accept". You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie
settings" link at the bottom of every page.

**App.** The app does not use cookies. It stores a sign-in session token and your settings on your device. Firebase
stores an app instance identifier on your device for analytics and crash reports.

## Age restriction

Isomantika is intended only for people **18 years of age or older**. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under
18. If you believe a minor has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

## Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access rules ensure that each user can see only their
own readings. API keys for AI services are stored on the server and are never included in the app. No system is
perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.
