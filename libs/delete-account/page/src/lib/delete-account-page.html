Delete Your Account

How to delete your Isomantika account and all your data in it.

In the app

  1. Open Isomantika and go to Settings.
  2. In the Account section, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm the deletion.

If you signed in with Apple, the app will ask you to confirm with Apple first.

What is deleted

Your account and all your readings are deleted immediately and permanently. This can't be undone.
